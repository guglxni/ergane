## False Positive Patterns to Avoid
- Routine library upgrades without technical novelty.

## Preferred Claim Drafting Style
- USPTO claim format: one independent claim numbered 1, dependent claims 2–4.
- Use "A method comprising..." or "A system comprising..." language.
- Avoid claiming software per se; claim the technical method/system.
